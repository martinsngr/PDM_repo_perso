# study2d_gapRise — can the solver carry free water rising in the gaps? (2026-10-30)

**Why.** REV_lowpc was meant to show free water moving beside the wicking yarn. With change L (`porousFreeAirKrPorous`,
study 2c) that configuration is stable, but the gap water stays low: T12 (cut REV, K 1e-11, VG 1000 / 0.4, θ 40°) gap top
0.231 mm at 2 ms against a yarn front of 0.632 mm; job 3059 (full REV, no L) 0.25 mm at 6.5 ms against 0.97 mm. **The
question (user, 2026-10-30): can the solver support gap water rising significantly?** The yarn front may stay ahead. The
values are chosen to observe the phenomenon, they are not calibrated.

**Case.** The truncated REV of study 2c (`../study2c_nearSaturation/makeREVcut.sh`, run T11: first 0.7 mm of the REV_lowpc
mesh and initial fields, 91 × 35 × 20 cells, closed end at y = 0.7 mm; half crossing yarn 5 at y 0–0.16 mm, crossing yarn 6
from y 0.50 mm). Same BCs, keywords and initial state as T11 and T12. **Changes (`makeCases.sh`):** yarn K in `0/K`, VG pc0
and m, θ0, `porousFreeAirKrPorous on`, 3 ms, writes every 0.1 ms. Reference: T12.

**Solver:** `hybridPorousInterFoam` = of9-port `8a97d02` (A–L with the H fix), laptop, 4 cores per run. Function objects:
`tools/` (link to the study 2c copies, with `dsLocator`).

**Commands.** `./makeCases.sh`, `./runAll.sh S1_K1e-12_t40 S2_K1e-12_t30 S3_K1e-12_lowpc_t40`, later `./runAll.sh S4_K1e-12_t20` (rerunning a folder resumes
it), `../../.venv/bin/python analyse.py` (table in `summary.md`). Run folders are regenerated by `makeCases.sh`: not committed.

K 1e-12 lies inside the Gebart range of the yarn inputs (Kpar 4.2e-12, Kperp 9.2e-13; `../../NOTES_cases.md`), pc0 5000 Pa is

(`../../NOTES_solver.md`), θ 30° is not. S2 and S3 each differ from S1 by one change.

**Success criterion: gap top (free box cells, α > 0.5) at y ≥ 0.5 mm by 3 ms, stable.** That is past the widening behind
yarn 5 and up to crossing yarn 6.

| Observation | Conclusion | Next (with the user) |
|---|---|---|
| a set reaches 0.5 mm, stable | the solver carries a significant gap rise | that set goes to the full REV (`REV_gapflow`); S1 if S1 and S2 both pass (θ inside the checked range) |

## Results (interim, 2026-10-30 17:10: S1, S2 near 3 ms, S4, S5 running, S6 queued)

|---|---|---|---|---|
| gap top α > 0.5 / α > 0.1 [mm] | 0.19 / 0.25 | 0.21 / 0.29 | 0.19 / 0.27 | 0.21 / 0.29 |
| gap water [cells] | 1,178 | 1,826 | 1,306 | 1,505 |

| per-step max free \|U\| after 0.2 ms [m/s] | 2.1 | 2.9 | 2.0 | 3.6 |

All stable. **S1 prediction "gap water at 1 ms ≥ T12" fails**; lower K gives less gap water (not tested why; the yarns may feed the
gaps as well as drain them). S3 stopped at 1.62 ms to free its cores. dt median 1.15–1.49e-6 s.

**S5 at 0.5 ms:** the column does not climb: gap top 0.371 mm at 0.1 ms, 0.351 mm at 0.5 ms; gap water 10,817 → 6,287 cells, of which
~75 % went to the padding (1,598 cells) and out through the open top and bottom (1,358 cells), the yarns took ~660. Confounded by that
leak (the initial block touches the padding) and by the base pc. Below 0.3 mm not reached yet; "≥ 0.5 mm by 1 ms" looks like a fail.

**Map of S2 at 1 ms (y-planes):** for y < 0.16 mm the water sits in narrow slots between yarn 5 and the y-yarns. Past the end of yarn 5 the
free space opens into gaps of about 8 cells between the y-yarn flanks, open at both z sides; there only a partly filled film (α 0.1–0.5)
creeps up the flanks, no bridge.

**The gap water follows the yarn saturation (measured, `satGap.py`).** S_eq = saturation where the yarn pc equals the gap suction
2σcosθ/w. In job 3059 (VG 1000 / 0.4, θ 40°, S_eq 0.932 at 300 µm, 0.841 at 160 µm) the gap top stays between the heights where the
y-yarns reach S_eq for the two widths:

| t [ms] | gap top [mm] | y-yarn at S_eq, 300 µm [mm] | y-yarn at S_eq, 160 µm [mm] | y-yarn front [mm] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.190 | 0.130 | 0.231 | 0.511 |
| 2 | 0.231 | 0.190 | 0.271 | 0.632 |
| 4 | 0.251 | 0.231 | 0.311 | 0.792 |
| 6.5 | 0.251 | 0.251 | 0.331 | 0.952 |

T12 agrees to 2 ms. Base pc (jobs 3051, 3055/3058, θ 60°): S_eq 0.995–0.998, the y-yarns reach 0.992 at the inlet at 30.5 ms, gap top 0.19 mm
apart from 2–4 corner cells at y 1.23–1.43 mm. 1D similarity: y = 0.16 mm + k√t with k 2.21 mm/√s (S_eq 0.841) gives 0.23 / 0.26 / 0.30 /
0.34 mm at 1 / 2 / 4 / 6.5 ms, against the measured contour 0.231 / 0.271 / 0.311 / 0.331. **Correlation on one pc curve, plus base pc
consistent; no run has reached the second gap.**

**Hypothesis (user, not tested):** the gap fills up to the next crossing yarn and a second gap rise starts once the yarns around it are saturated.
