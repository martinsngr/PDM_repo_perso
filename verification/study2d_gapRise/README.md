# study2d_gapRise — can the solver carry free water rising in the gaps? (2026-10-30)

**Why.** REV_lowpc was meant to show free water moving beside the wicking yarn. With change L (`porousFreeAirKrPorous`,
study 2c) that configuration is stable, but the gap water stays low: T12 (cut REV, K 1e-11, VG 1000 / 0.4, θ 40°) gap top
0.231 mm at 2 ms against a yarn front of 0.632 mm; job 3059 (full REV, no L) 0.25 mm at 6.5 ms against 0.97 mm. **The
question (user, 2026-10-30): can the solver support gap water rising significantly?** The yarn front may stay ahead. The
values are chosen to observe the phenomenon, they are not calibrated.

**Case.** The truncated REV of study 2c (`../study2c_nearSaturation/makeREVcut.sh`, run T11: first 0.7 mm of the REV_lowpc
mesh and initial fields, 91 × 35 × 20 cells, closed end at y = 0.7 mm; half crossing yarn 5 at y 0–0.16 mm, crossing yarn 6
from y 0.50 mm). Same BCs, keywords and initial state as T11 and T12. **Changes (`makeCases.sh`):** yarn K in `0/K`, VG pc0
and m, θ0, `porousFreeAirKrPorous on`, 3 ms, writes every 0.1 ms. Reference: T12.

**Solver:** `hybridPorousInterFoam` = of9-port `8a97d02` (A–L with the H fix), laptop, 4 cores per run. Function objects:
`tools/` (link to the study 2c copies, with `dsLocator`).

**Commands.** `./makeCases.sh`, `./runAll.sh S1_K1e-12_t40 S2_K1e-12_t30 S3_K1e-12_lowpc_t40` (rerunning a folder resumes
it), `../../.venv/bin/python analyse.py` (table in `summary.md`). Run folders are regenerated by `makeCases.sh`: not committed.

## Estimates behind the sets (no run at K 1e-12 before this study)
Yarn front in the REV = 1D similarity solution × 13.9/18.04 (the REV/1D ratio of job 3051; it predicts job 3059 within
2 %). Gap drain time = (w/2A)², A the 1D uptake rate: the time the yarn walls take to absorb the volume of a gap of width w.

| Set | K [m²] | pc0 [Pa] / m | θ | yarn front [mm/√s] | A [mm/√s] | drain time, w 300 µm | yarn S where pc = gap suction (300 / 160 µm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T12 (reference) | 1e-11 | 1000 / 0.4 | 40° | 8.55 | 3.27 | 2.1 ms | 0.932 / 0.841 |
| S1 | 1e-12 | 5000 / 0.5 | 40° | 4.40 | 1.93 | 6.0 ms | 0.996 / 0.990 |
| S2 | 1e-12 | 5000 / 0.5 | 30° | 4.40 | 1.93 | 6.0 ms | 0.996 / 0.987 |
| S3 | 1e-12 | 1000 / 0.4 | 40° | 2.70 | 1.03 | 21 ms | 0.932 / 0.841 |

K 1e-12 lies inside the Gebart range of the yarn inputs (Kpar 4.2e-12, Kperp 9.2e-13; `../../NOTES_cases.md`), pc0 5000 Pa is
the physical order; not Leverett-consistent (K/10 would give pc0 ≈ 16 kPa). θ 40° and 60° are checked on the slit
(`../../NOTES_solver.md`), θ 30° is not. S2 and S3 each differ from S1 by one change.

## Predictions (written before the runs)
**Main risk:** the valve behind a crossing yarn depends on the geometry and θ, not on K; at K 1e-12 the water may just pile
up. Hypothesis, not tested: concave 90° staircase corners wick below θ 45° (Concus–Finn), which would fit "60° holds, 40°
passes".

| Run | Prediction |
|---|---|
| all | stable: no step above 5 m/s after 0.2 ms, per-step max free \|U\| ≤ 3 m/s from 0.6 ms, no stopGuard, budget ≤ 1e-8 of the water |
| `S1_K1e-12_t40` | dt median 5e-7 to 1.25e-6 s; y-yarn front 0.45–0.55 mm at 3 ms; gap water at 1 ms ≥ T12 (1,505 cells); **gap top ≥ 0.30 mm at 3 ms** (T12: 0.231 mm at 2 ms), more likely below 0.5 mm than above |
| `S2_K1e-12_t30` | as S1; gap top at 3 ms at least 0.04 mm (2 cells) above S1 |
| `S3_K1e-12_lowpc_t40` | y-yarn front 0.35–0.45 mm at 3 ms; gap water at 3 ms ≥ 1.2 × S1; gap top ≥ S1; dt median ≥ 5e-7 s |

**Success criterion: gap top (free box cells, α > 0.5) at y ≥ 0.5 mm by 3 ms, stable.** That is past the widening behind
yarn 5 and up to crossing yarn 6.

| Observation | Conclusion | Next (with the user) |
|---|---|---|
| a set reaches 0.5 mm, stable | the solver carries a significant gap rise | that set goes to the full REV (`REV_gapflow`); S1 if S1 and S2 both pass (θ inside the checked range) |
| none reaches 0.5 mm, top still rising at 3 ms (≥ 0.04 mm/ms over the last ms) | slow rise | continue the best set on the full REV |
| top flat below 0.3 mm in all | the valve does not depend on K | locate where the water stops; θ 20°, a lower K |
| a set is not stable | L is not enough at those values | cause first (see `../../NOTES_solver.md`, open issues), no rerun |
